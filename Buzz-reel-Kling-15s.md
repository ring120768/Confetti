# Buzz Reel — Kling 15-Second Segments (stitch together)

Revised from the 30s pack. Four **15-second** clips, each a **single continuous scene** with a short line of voiceover, stitched in your editor. Runs ~60s full — or drop a middle segment for a ~45s or ~30s cut.

## Why the last one rushed (and the fix)
Kling speeds the voice up to fit whatever text you give it into the clip. **The fix is less script per clip.** Each segment below keeps the VO to ~22–25 words so it lands at a calm pace over 15 seconds. Even better, see "Cleaner audio" at the bottom.

## Settings (every clip)
- Model **Kling 2.x**, **9:16**, **duration 15s** (if your version maxes at 10s, use 10s and trim the VO lines by a few words)
- Image-to-video for the Buzz shots, using a **single cropped pose** from `brand-assets/buzz-character-refs/`
- Negative prompt (paste every time):
```
on-screen text, captions, letters, watermark, ui, app screenshot, distorted hands, extra fingers, deformed faces, blurry, low quality, realistic insect, creepy, uncanny, morphing, flicker, multiple bees
```

## Stitching (CapCut / editor)
1. Line the 4 clips up in order; trim any awkward frames at the joins.
2. Add a soft cross-dissolve or a quick confetti wipe between clips so the cuts feel intentional.
3. Keep one music bed running underneath the whole thing (don't let each clip's audio restart it).
4. Burn in the captions per segment; hold the final CTA ~2s.
5. Export 1080×1920, 9:16.

## Cleaner audio (recommended)
For flawless pacing, **mute Kling's generated voice** and instead lay **one continuous voiceover** across the stitched video — record it yourself, or use a text-to-speech voice. Then Kling only has to make the visuals, and the narration never rushes. This is how most polished reels are made.
