# Buzz the Wedding Fairy — Kling Prompt Pack

Buzz flies through a wedding scene like Tinker Bell, trailing fairy-dust and magically making the day appear. Whimsical, warm, brand-pastel.

## How to run it (reminder)
- **One prompt = one clip.** Paste only the *prompt paragraph* into Kling.
- **Image-to-video** from a single cropped Buzz pose (`brand-assets/buzz-character-refs/` → use a **flying/side pose**, cleanest for motion).
- **9:16** for reels (switch to **16:9** for the website hero).
- **Duration 10s** (Kling's magic reveals look best with time to breathe).
- **Keep it to ONE or TWO reveals per clip** — ask Kling to materialise five things at once and it gets messy. Stitch several clips for more.

## Negative prompt (paste every time)
```
on-screen text, letters, watermark, distorted, extra limbs, deformed, realistic insect, creepy, uncanny, flicker, morphing, multiple bees, blurry, low quality, cluttered
```

**Reference:** Buzz flying / side pose.

Whimsical enchanting style, cinematic, shallow depth of field, gentle magical motion.

## Stitch idea (~30–40s)
Hero cake → arch → table → dance-floor finale. Add a soft, sparkly music bed and a light "shimmer" sound on each reveal. End on your logo + **Where every detail sparkles ✨** and the URL.

## Tips for believable magic
- Words that trigger the effect well in Kling: *materialises, blooms, in her wake, trail of fairy-dust, swirl of sparkles, one by one*.
- Keep the **camera following Buzz** — a moving camera sells the fairy energy far better than a locked-off shot.
- Reuse the **same Buzz crop** across all four so she stays identical.
- If a reveal looks muddy, simplify to a single object and regenerate — one clean piece of magic beats three messy ones.
