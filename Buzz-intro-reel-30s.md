## Production notes
- **First 1.5 seconds must earn the scroll** — lead with the couple + the hook line on screen; don't open on the logo.
- Keep every clip **1.5–2.5s** — pace is everything on reels.
- Show **real screens** of the app wherever possible (timeline ticking, Buzz replying, the Delegate "Sent to Mum ✨"). Authentic app footage outperforms slick mockups here.
- Buzz should **react** — little wing flutter, a wink at the CTA — she's the personality.
- End on the tagline held for ~2s so it lands and the CTA is readable.
- Record the VO warm and unhurried even though it's quick — Buzz is never frantic; she's the calm in the chaos.
