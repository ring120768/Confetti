# Buzz 30s Reel — Kling AI Prompt Pack

Six 5-second clips = 30 seconds. Built for Kling 2.x, **9:16 vertical**.

## Read this first (what Kling can and can't do)
- **Kling is for the emotional / lifestyle b-roll and Buzz herself** — couples, hands, confetti, the mascot.
- **Do NOT ask Kling to render app screens or text** — it garbles UI and letters. For the timeline, chat and delegate moments, use a **real screen-recording of the app** and composite it onto the phone in your editor (CapCut/Premiere).
- **Captions + Buzz's voiceover are added in post**, not by Kling. Kling can't reliably lip-sync a mascot from a prompt, so record Buzz's VO separately and lay it over the cut.
- **Keep Buzz consistent:** use **image-to-video** with the *same* reference image for every Buzz shot. Your character sheets are saved in `brand-assets/buzz-character-refs/` (`buzz-character-sheet-1.png`, `buzz-character-sheet-2.png`). ⚠️ **Crop out a single pose** before using as a reference — Kling image-to-video wants ONE bee on a clean background, not the whole multi-pose sheet. Suggested crops: the **front-on smiling** pose for Clip 2, and the **winking** pose for the Clip 6 CTA.

## Global settings
- Model: **Kling 2.1 / 2.0**, Professional/High-Quality mode
- Aspect ratio: **9:16** · Duration: **5s** · Motion: moderate · CFG ≈ 0.5

## Global negative prompt (paste into every clip)
```
on-screen text, captions, subtitles, letters, watermark, ui, app interface, distorted hands, extra fingers, deformed faces, blurry, low quality, oversaturated, realistic insect, creepy, uncanny, morphing, flicker
```

## Assembly checklist (CapCut / Premiere)
1. Drop the 6 clips in order; trim each to ~1.5–2.5s of the best motion (you don't need all 5s).
2. Composite the real app screen-recordings onto the phones in clips 3–5 (mask/track to the screen).
3. Lay Buzz's VO across the whole thing; add the burned-in captions per clip.
4. Add an upbeat, feel-good music bed; duck it slightly under the VO.
5. Hold the final CTA card ~2s so the URL is readable.
6. Export **1080×1920, 9:16**.

## Tip for character consistency
Generate **Clip 2 first**, pick the best Buzz frame, and reuse *that exact frame* as the reference image for Clip 6 — keeps her looking identical top and tail.
