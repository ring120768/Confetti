# Wedding Planner Pro — HeyGen Presenter Script (v2, different features)

**Length:** ~55 seconds · **Read by:** your HeyGen avatar (or Buzz's voice over app b-roll)
**Tone:** warm, friendly, confident — a helpful friend, not a hard sell. British English.
**Feature focus (deliberately different from the reel):** cost-vs-planner hook · dated plan + calendar sync · AI supplier research with real reviews + enquiry emails · budget tracking · delegation · founding offer.

Paste each scene's **Script** into a HeyGen scene; use the **On screen** notes for b-roll / captions.

### Scene 1 — Hook
**Script:** "A wedding planner can cost thousands. What if you had one in your pocket… for about the price of a coffee?"
**On screen:** presenter to camera → cut to the app opening on the cream home screen.

### Scene 2 — The plan + calendar
**Script:** "Wedding Planner Pro builds your whole plan around your date — hundreds of tasks, each one timed so nothing ever sneaks up on you. And it syncs straight into your phone's calendar."
**On screen:** the dated task timeline; a task ticking off; then the plan appearing in a phone calendar.

### Scene 3 — Ask Buzz (supplier research)
**Script:** "Need a venue or a photographer? Just ask Buzz, our AI planner. She finds real suppliers near you, checks the reviews, and even writes the enquiry emails — you just hit send."
**On screen:** Buzz chat searching venues; a shortlist with star ratings; a drafted email.

### Scene 4 — Budget, guests, delegate
**Script:** "Track every penny in the budget planner, keep your whole guest list in order, and hand jobs to the bridal party with a single tap."
**On screen:** budget bar filling; guest RSVPs; the Delegate button → "Sent to Mum ✨".

### Scene 5 — Close / CTA
**Script:** "It's free to start — and our founding couples get their first month completely free. Wedding Planner Pro. Where every detail sparkles."
**On screen:** logo on cream, confetti fall, App Store + Google Play badges, weddingplannerpro.co.uk.
